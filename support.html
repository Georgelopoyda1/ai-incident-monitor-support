HELP & SUPPORT

Answers, reporting, and troubleshooting.

AI Incident Monitor is a free iPhone app that gathers public reporting about AI-related incidents and potential harms. Use it to investigate linked sources, not as a verified emergency-warning system.

How do I refresh reports?

Open the app and tap the circular refresh symbol in the top-right corner, or pull down on the dashboard. The app requests reports from public online sources. Refresh may be slower when a source is unavailable; its status is listed under Internet source health.

What does the concern percentage mean?

The percentage is a rule-based reporting-concern index, not the probability of an AI takeover or a real-world danger percentage. It gives more weight to severe, recent reports covered by different publishers. It can still contain false positives, reporting bias and incomplete information. Tap “Why N%? See the evidence” to inspect the contributing source links.

How do warnings work?

Tap Enable iPhone alerts and grant iOS notification permission. The app can create a local notification when a permitted refresh discovers an eligible new report. You may also enable early warnings for single-source stories; those will be labelled unverified. Use Send test notification to check permission. iOS background execution is not guaranteed on a fixed schedule, so the app cannot promise instant 24/7 alerts while closed.

What if no sources respond?

Check your phone's internet connection and try a manual refresh. Previously downloaded reports can still appear offline. A missing or stale connection does not mean that no AI-related incident has occurred.

Are these incidents verified?

Articles and source matches are leads, not forensic findings. The app may check that an original public document contains topic-specific text; this verifies that the page is accessible and discusses the subject, not every factual claim on the page. Always consult linked primary sources.

Is there a subscription or sign-up?

No. The app is free, with no in-app purchase, sign-in, subscription, advertising or developer-hosted analytics in the current release.

Contact support or report an issue

Visit the public support issue tracker. To submit a new report, open a GitHub support issue. Please do not include personal contact details, passwords, private files or other sensitive information because GitHub issues are public. You can also read our privacy policy.
