PRIVACY POLICY

Your data stays on your device.

Effective November 13, 2026. This policy covers the AI Incident Monitor iPhone app.

Information the developer collects

The app does not require an account. The current version does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising SDKs, user tracking, a sign-in service, or a developer-hosted server that receives your use of the app. The developer does not collect your name, email address, contact list, location, or personal health information through the app.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app saves downloaded public report titles, links, publisher information, source check status, original-document verification results, locally generated warning history, and your optional notification preferences on your device. This information is used to display the app and retain reports for offline viewing. Removing the app and its data through iOS removes the locally stored records, subject to Apple's own device-backup behavior.

Connections to independent websites

When you open the app or iOS runs a permitted background refresh, your iPhone contacts third-party public resources such as Google News RSS, the AI Incident Database, provider status feeds, and websites containing articles or reference documents. Those third parties may receive standard network request information including your IP address, device/network metadata and access times under their own privacy practices. Opening external links in the browser likewise contacts the destination site. This policy does not control those third parties.

Notifications

If you opt in, the app may schedule local device notifications for newly discovered reports. Notification permission and delivery are controlled by iOS. You can disable notifications under Settings → Notifications or through the app. Notification delivery while the app is closed depends on iOS background refresh and is not guaranteed to be immediate.

Data sharing and retention

The app does not sell personal information or send your saved archive to the developer. Locally stored reports persist until the app deletes them under its retention rules or you remove app data. Public report links and online source requests are handled by the respective external websites. The app does not offer a developer account or cloud-sync service.

Children and suitability

The app presents public news and cybersecurity-related material, which may discuss safety incidents, crime or other troubling subjects. It is intended for general informational use, not for children under 13.

Changes

Any material changes in data collection or sharing will be reflected in an updated policy on this page before a corresponding release.

Contact and questions

For privacy questions or support, use the public support page or submit a support issue on GitHub. Do not post personal identifiers, account credentials, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
