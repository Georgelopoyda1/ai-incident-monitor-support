One clear view of AI-related reporting

The AI Incident Concern Index shows an algorithm-defined percentage based on severity, recency and corroboration of selected news reports. It is not a statistical probability of AI causing harm, escaping control or taking over the world.

Important: A news headline is not proof. A multi-publisher report confirms similar coverage, not necessarily the accuracy of a claim or the cause of an incident. The app is an information tool—not an emergency alert service or verified threat detector.

Designed for transparency

  • Twenty public feed connections spanning fourteen AI-risk topics, subject to source availability.
  • Source links, publication dates, feed health and offline access to saved reports.
  • Optional local notifications when the app detects newly reported stories. iOS may delay or skip background fetches; notifications are not guaranteed to be immediate.
  • Original-document text checks when pages are accessible, with failures shown instead of hidden.
